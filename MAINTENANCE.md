# Where this project stands

**Active development by the original author ends here.**

Sakura Crossing is finished as a one-person project. It is not abandoned, it is
not broken, and it is not going anywhere — it is simply at the point where one
person has taken it as far as one person reasonably can. Everything in it works
and is documented.

**The repository stays open, and contributions are genuinely welcome.** If you
want to keep making it better, you do not need to ask permission and you do not
need to wait for a reply. Fork it, open a pull request, open an issue, or just
build your own version on top of it. That is what the GPL is for.

## What you are inheriting

One HTML file, about 800 KB, no build step, no image assets, no CDN. A Japanese
neighbourhood at dusk that you can walk, drive and swim around: a railway with a
level crossing and two houses the train runs through, a river with a bascule
bridge that opens for ferries, a viaduct that climbs from the street to the
elevated expressway, a sewer you can walk through and climb out of, six
residents, a drivable kei car, and a day/night cycle.

It renders with a custom cel-shading pipeline on three.js r128 — a
normals-and-depth prepass, Roberts-cross ink outlines, bloom and ACES tone
mapping, all in one composite pass.

## Three things worth knowing before you change anything

**The engine is three.js r128, and that is load-bearing.** No colour
management, no TSL, no WebGPU. Several things that are one line in a modern
build are done by hand here. Upgrading is a real project, not a version bump —
the toon gradient map, the manual sRGB linearisation and the whole composite
pass all depend on r128 behaviour.

**The ground is a level-aware grid, not a heightmap.** Walkable surfaces carry a
height and obstacles carry a height *range*, because the world has decks over
streets and a sewer under them. `heightAt(x, z, y)` returns the surface within
reach of the asker, not the highest one; `blocked(x, z, r, y)` ignores walls
that are entirely above or below you. Most collision bugs in this project's
history came from forgetting that one of those arguments exists.

**Verify behaviour, not appearance.** `tools/walktest.mjs` measures whether the
character climbs the kerb, whether walls stop them, and whether W goes where the
camera looks. A screenshot cannot tell you any of that. When something animates,
freeze the camera, step the clock by hand and diff two frames — the number of
"it looks fine" bugs caught this way is not small.

## Ideas that are mapped but not built

- **Mobile.** Touch controls are already in the source. The blocker is
  performance: roughly 4500 draw calls per frame, and the scene is traversed
  three times. Merging static geometry is the win, and it has to exclude
  everything that moves.
- **Winding rivers.** The channel is a rectangle today, and the ground hole, the
  water, the banks, the barriers and the bridge alignment all depend on that.
  Making it meander means reworking all five together.
- **Driving underwater**, interiors you can enter, traffic on the expressway
  deck, and sound beyond the crossing bell.
