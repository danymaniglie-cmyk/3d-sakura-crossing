# Sakura Crossing

A Japanese neighbourhood at dusk, in 3D, that you can walk around. Cartoon /
cel-shaded, cut in two by a railway with a level crossing.

**One HTML file. No build step. No image assets. No CDN.** Every texture — roof
tiles, plaster, asphalt, the 2048×512 train livery with its lit windows and
passenger silhouettes — is painted onto a canvas at load time. That is why the
whole thing is 800 KB.

## Run it

```bash
python3 -m http.server 5191
```

Then open **http://localhost:5191/index.html**

Double-clicking the file will not work: `file://` rules block loading
`vendor/three.min.js`.

| key | |
|---|---|
| `WASD` | walk |
| `Shift` | run |
| mouse drag | look |
| `V` / `F5` | first / third person |
| `E` | talk to whoever is nearby |

## What's in there

- A main street with raised pavements, kerbs, tactile paving and drains
- A level crossing: barriers that come down, alternating red lights, the bell
- Ten two-storey houses. Each with a plinth, a tiled roof with ridge caps,
  onigawara and a gable board, exposed rafters under the eaves, gutters and
  downpipes, a TV aerial and a satellite dish, windows with frames, sills,
  sliding shutters and planters, an entrance with a step, noren, a nameplate,
  a canopy and a lit lantern, stepping stones, a balcony with futons hung out,
  an air conditioner, a meter, a fence and a mailbox
- **Two of those houses are built over the railway**: the track runs through a
  brick arch at their base and the house carries on above it
- An elevated expressway with a tiled underpass, and a helical ramp climbing to
  the deck at a constant 4.9% grade
- A shopping strip: supermarket, konbini, estate agent, pachinko parlour
- Cherry trees, hedges, boundary walls, power poles with catenary wires, street
  lamps, vending machines, bicycles, kei cars, a bus stop, a jizō shrine,
  hanging lanterns, a cat on a wall, birds, fireflies, falling petals
- Six residents who walk about, stop when the train comes, look at you as you
  pass, and can be talked to
- A day/night cycle

## How it works

**Cel shading.** A toon gradient map with three hard bands. Ink outlines are not
inflated duplicate meshes but a composition pass: a normals-and-depth prepass, a
Roberts cross over both, then bloom and ACES tone mapping. Uniform across the
whole scene for the cost of one extra pass.

**Solid ground.** Walkable surfaces carry a height; obstacles block. You step up
onto a kerb if the rise is under 0.55 m. Movement resolves on X and then Z
separately, so you slide along a wall instead of sticking to it.

**Verification.** `tools/walktest.mjs` measures behaviour, not appearance — a
screenshot cannot tell you whether the character climbs the kerb or walks
through it.

```bash
node tools/walktest.mjs
```

## It is on three.js r128, and that matters

Three constraints that come from the version, and that will cost you hours if
you change them without knowing why:

- The toon gradient map must be **`RGBAFormat`** with the grey replicated across
  all three channels. r128 samples it with `.rgb`; in `RedFormat` green and blue
  stay at zero and **the entire scene renders red**.
- r128 has no automatic colour management. Colours are written as sRGB hex and
  linearised by hand with `convertSRGBToLinear()` — including the ones
  reassigned every frame. Skip it and everything looks bleached.
- Three toon bands, `[76, 150, 255]`. Four softens the shading and the cartoon
  read disappears.
